Kacie & Clancy

A backyard wedding in New Hampshire · September 2026

A September afternoon in the backyard of Clancy’s childhood home: wide-open views and a family that never stopped helping one another.

01

Home

A tent on the lawn, hydrangeas on every table, and a few personal touches that were pure Kacie and Clancy.

Getting ready

The flower girls led the way, Kacie walked in with her parents, and Clancy didn’t try to hide a thing.
